## Lumtub 0.2.10

### Explore
- **The language filter goes by the language a video is spoken in**, not its title. YouTube machine-translates listed titles into English, so filtering used to keep Chinese videos with English titles and drop ones whose titles were translated. Videos that pass show the title their uploader wrote. Each video is checked once and the answer is remembered.
- Filtered results fill in faster: several videos are checked at once and each one shows as soon as its answer is in.
- **More regions and languages:** about 110 regions grouped by continent, and 35 languages.

### Subscriptions
- **Channels streaming live now are marked**: they come first in the channel strip with a red ring and a "直播" tag, and live videos lead the subscription feed.
