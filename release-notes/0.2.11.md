## Lumtub 0.2.11

### Playback
- **The picture recovers on its own when the graphics driver resets.** A reset used to leave the video flickering or black with sound still playing; Lumtub now makes a new video output and picks up where it was. If the driver keeps resetting, hardware decoding is turned off for the session, and after a third reset Lumtub asks you to restart it.

### Subtitles
- **Switching or turning off the second subtitle takes effect at once.** Its old line no longer stays on screen until the first subtitle is changed.

### Settings
- More settings now apply at once instead of after a restart or the next video: SponsorBlock and its categories, the libass subtitle switch, and subtitle style with mpv drawing subtitles.
- Choosing 自动 quality lifts the cap set after dropped frames.
- Custom mpv options you remove go back to their defaults.
- Changes to decoding and frame interpolation say they apply from the next video.

### Comments and posts
- **Comments, posts and live chat can be selected and copied.**

### New: hide AI-made videos
- 设置 now has **屏蔽 AI 生成的视频** (off by default). It can hide videos YouTube labels as made with AI, and optionally channels on the AI channel lists of KillSlop (killslop.app, CC BY-SA 4.0) and AiSList (CC BY-NC 4.0), downloaded once a day. Channels you subscribe to or follow are never hidden.
