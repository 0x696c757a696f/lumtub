## Lumtub 0.2.12

### Explore by language
- **Picking a language in Explore finds videos in it.** The zones used to search with Chinese words, so other languages found next to nothing. Search words are now translated word by word into the language picked, else the region's, else the interface's. Taiwan and Hong Kong search in traditional characters. Zones you added yourself are searched as you wrote them and never sent to be translated.
- **Videos are judged by the language spoken in them,** from the original audio track or the automatic captions, and from the title and description only when neither is there. Italian, Dutch, Polish, Turkish and the other Latin-script languages are now told apart.
- 全球热门 keeps the language you picked; only the region is left out.
- New **只看确认的原声** chip: only videos whose audio or captions confirm the language.
- When nothing matches, the page says how many videos were checked and offers **继续找**.

### Playback
- **A picture that freezes while the sound plays on now comes back by itself.** When a stream breaks off or the video data stops decoding, Lumtub opens the video again where it was.
- **VP9 is no longer turned off by mistake.** One video decoded in software, for example after a graphics driver reset, used to switch the automatic VP9 off for good. What your graphics card can decode is now checked at every start.

### Subtitles
- The subtitle panel lists the original language first, then your interface language, then your system language, each once.
- 字幕列表 shows the original lines with the translation under them when only a translated subtitle is on, and always knows when two subtitles are on.
- The chapter menu can translate chapter names (翻译章节名).

### Logs
- **Log levels:** 设置 › 关于 › 日志级别 picks errors, warnings, info (default) or debug. The log page can filter by level.
