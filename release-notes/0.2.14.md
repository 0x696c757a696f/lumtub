## Playback

- Fewer interruptions: streams are now downloaded in 10 MiB pieces. YouTube cut the single long download every couple of megabytes, and after five cuts in a row playback stopped and reopened.
- Live streams opened from a list that did not mark them live are now recognized: the live chat connects, picture and sound come from one stream so they stay in sync, and playback starts at the live edge.
- Switching videos quickly no longer records the earlier video in the history or prefetches its next video.

## Under the hood

- The local stream relay limits connections, idle time and request line length.
- Resolved streams are reused only through the proxy they were resolved with.
