## Playback

- Far fewer interruptions with subtitles on. Videos with subtitles now stream through Lumtub's local relay too, which downloads each chunk in full on its own instead of letting the player read YouTube slowly until the connection is cut.
- When a subtitle shown by itself has to be translated, the video waits for the translation before it starts (10 seconds at most) and shows "Translating subtitles…". Videos without subtitles, or with subtitles already in the language wanted, start right away.
- A mouse click on the picture pauses, and another click plays again.

## Subtitles

- The second subtitle follows the interface language by default.
- When the first and second subtitle would be in the same language, only the second one is shown.

## Playlists

- Videos played from a playlist show the playlist beside the player, with the current video highlighted.
- Playlists can be played In order or Reversed; the choice is remembered per playlist.

## Fixes

- Live streams that have ended no longer show "live now" in History, Continue watching and Recently watched.
- The comment toolbar wraps on narrow windows instead of cutting off the sort buttons.
