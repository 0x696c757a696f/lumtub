## Explore

- For You ranks every video gathered so far instead of each page on its own. It reads a few pages ahead, so the best matches come first; what is already shown never moves.
- The language filter asks YouTube for titles and descriptions in the language you picked. A video whose uploader translated its title into English is no longer taken for an English video when nothing else tells. Videos judged English by their title alone before are checked once more.

## Under the hood

- The app's main controller is split into smaller files (explore, subtitles, chat, picture health, settings, navigation). Nothing changes in how the app behaves.
