## Lumtub 0.2.2

### New
- **Automatic updates.** Lumtub checks for a new version at startup and every 6 hours, downloads it in the background, verifies its SHA-256 and installs it silently after you close Lumtub, so nothing you are watching is interrupted. Settings › About › Check for updates checks right away, shows download progress and can restart to update at once. Turn off **Update Lumtub automatically** to only be told about new versions. Updates start from this version; 0.2.1 and older need this one installed by hand.
- **Simplified Chinese interface.** Lumtub follows the Windows display language: simplified Chinese for Chinese, English otherwise. Settings › Appearance › Language changes it. The log stays in English. Translations are now JSON files anyone can add to; see TRANSLATING.md.
- **Explore › Global trending:** the most watched videos across zones with no region or language filter, up to 2 per channel.
- **New creators** mode in every video zone: this week's videos with under 5,000 views from channels you have not watched or followed.

### Changed
- **Faster search and channel pages.** Search results and a channel's Videos, Shorts and Live tabs are read straight from YouTube instead of through yt-dlp, which took a second or two just to start. yt-dlp is still used when YouTube's answer cannot be read.
- **Smoother high-resolution playback.** The picture is now drawn at the size it is shown instead of at the video's own size and scaled down afterwards, so a 4K video in a window no longer makes the GPU draw 4K frames. The local relay that fetches YouTube streams in pieces, and the bridge that lets libmpv use a SOCKS proxy, now run in the native core, so the video's data no longer passes through the interface's thread.
- **Codecs follow your GPU.** At startup Lumtub asks the graphics driver which codecs it decodes in hardware, and remembers the answer until the graphics card or its driver changes. VP9 and AV1 are disabled unless your GPU decodes them, so playback stays light. Above 1080p, which H.264 cannot reach, VP9 stays enabled even in software, and Lumtub warns that it will use the CPU. When the tallest version of a video exists only in a disabled codec, Lumtub says which quality it plays instead. Choose codecs yourself and Lumtub keeps your choice; choose the same as the automatic one and it stays automatic. H.264 cannot be disabled when neither VP9 nor AV1 is decoded in hardware.
- At least one of H.264, VP9 and AV1 always stays enabled.
- More of Lumtub runs in its native core (`lumtub_core.dll`): block lists, the Shorts test, title-language and bait detection, the download dialog's format choice, the update check, log redaction, SponsorBlock and DeArrow lookups, and reading YouTube's related videos, comments, community posts and live chat. Large YouTube responses are no longer decoded on the UI thread, and downloaded components are verified on a background thread.

## Lumtub 0.2.2（中文）

- **搜索和频道页更快**：搜索结果和频道的视频、Shorts、直播页直接向 YouTube 读取，不再每次启动 yt-dlp（光启动就要一两秒）；读不出来时仍自动改用 yt-dlp。
- **自动更新**：启动时和每 6 小时检查新版本，后台下载并校验 SHA-256，关闭 Lumtub 后静默安装，不打断观看。设置 › 关于 › 检查更新可立即检查、查看下载进度并立即重启更新。关闭“自动更新 Lumtub”后只提示不下载。从这个版本开始生效，0.2.1 及更早版本需要手动安装本版。
- **简体中文界面**：界面语言跟随 Windows，中文系统显示简体中文，其他显示英文；可在设置 › 外观设置 › 语言里更改。日志仍为英文。翻译改为 JSON 文件，任何人都可以参与，见 TRANSLATING.md。
- **探索 › 全球热门**：不限地区和语言的热门视频，每个频道最多 2 个。
- **新人新作**模式：各视频分区里本周新发、播放不到 5000、来自你没看过也没关注的频道的视频。
- **高分辨率播放更流畅**：画面改为按显示大小渲染，不再先按视频原尺寸画再缩小。窗口里看 4K 视频，显卡不用再每帧画 4K。分段读取 YouTube 视频流的本地中转，以及让 libmpv 使用 SOCKS 代理的桥接，也改由原生核心处理，视频数据不再经过界面线程。
- **编码跟着显卡走**：启动时向显卡驱动询问能硬解哪些编码，结果记住，换显卡或更新驱动后才重新检测。显卡不能硬解的 VP9、AV1 默认禁用，播放保持低占用。1080p 以上 H.264 到不了，这时 VP9 即使只能软解也会启用，并提示会用 CPU。视频的最高画质只有被禁用的编码时，会提示实际播放的画质。自己选的编码设置会保留；和自动结果一样的，仍按自动处理。VP9 和 AV1 都不能硬解时，不能禁用 H.264。
- H.264、VP9、AV1 至少保留一个启用。
- 更多功能改由原生核心（`lumtub_core.dll`）处理：屏蔽列表、Shorts 判断、标题语言和标题党识别、下载格式选择、更新检查、日志脱敏、SponsorBlock 和 DeArrow 查询，以及相关视频、评论、社区帖子和直播聊天的解析。YouTube 的大响应不再在界面线程里解码，组件下载后的校验也放到后台线程。
