## Members-only videos

- Members-only videos carry a green Members only mark on their cards in search, channel and related lists. Lumtub no longer preloads them when the pointer rests on one.
- If you are a member and signed in under Settings › YouTube account › Browser sign-in, members-only videos play. The sign-in's cookies are used only for these videos: they are written to a private file for that one lookup and wiped as soon as it ends. Every other video still plays without them.
- When a members-only video can't play, the watch page says why: sign in under Browser sign-in, or the signed-in account isn't a member of that channel.

## Fixes

- Closing Lumtub could end in an "application error" box. The video output is now released while the app is still running, so the window closes cleanly.

## Blocking

- The Falun Gong groups also hide more NTD and Epoch Times programmes, Gan Jing World, Sound of Hope, Epoch Health and hosts who work with these outlets (Chen Pokong, Zhang Tianliang, Shi Tao and others).

## Under the hood

- What a resolved video offers (picture heights per allowed codec, when its addresses expire, whether it plays straight from its addresses) is now worked out in the Rust core. Nothing changes in how playback behaves.
