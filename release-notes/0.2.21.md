## Linux

- Lumtub now runs on Linux as an AppImage, for x64 and arm64. Download it, make it executable and open it; it updates itself like the Windows version. It needs a system from 2023 or later (Debian 12, Ubuntu 24.04, Fedora 38 or newer).
- Media keys, the tray icon (where the desktop has one), close to tray, keep on top (also on KDE Wayland) and hardware decoding through VA-API all work.

## Live streams

- Live streams above 1080p play: when a live offers 1440p or 4K, as in the browser, Lumtub plays it too. If that stream breaks right away, the live plays up to 1080p as before. If automatic quality stays at 1080p, pick 1440p or 2160p in the quality menu.
- A Live mark sits where the time is shown. It turns into Back to live once you fall behind; clicking it, or pressing End, jumps to the newest moment.

## Casting

- Casting to a TV plays up to 1080p, and up to 4K or 8K when you choose it under Cast quality. Before, a TV only got 360p.
- A cast keeps playing through dropped connections and through stream addresses that expire during a long video.

## Faster start

- A video can start before yt-dlp has finished resolving it: Lumtub asks YouTube directly at the same time and plays whichever answer comes first.

## Looks

- A new minimal look: neutral surfaces, thin borders instead of shadows, no ripples.
- Ten colour palettes, light and dark, and a warm light for the evening, on always, from sunset to sunrise or between set hours. Dark mode can follow the sun or set hours too.
- Compact mode for tighter lists and menus, list and text-only views for video lists, and a low resource mode.
- Your YouTube account picture shows on the account button, in account settings and on your own comments.
- Selected filter chips no longer turn into blank white boxes.

## Languages

- The interface is available in 72 languages.

## Fixes

- The chosen interface language, a pending brightness change and the second subtitle size are kept after a restart.
- Video brightness stays between -50 and 50, so the picture never goes all black or all white.

## Under the hood

- Where certificates come from can be chosen under Playback settings: the system's, Mozilla's or Google's.
