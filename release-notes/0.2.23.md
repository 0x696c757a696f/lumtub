## Player

- The quality button and its menu name the frame rate the way YouTube does, for videos and live streams alike: 1080p60, 1440p50, 2160p60. Pictures at 30 frames a second or fewer keep the plain height, such as 720p. The download dialog shows the same labels.

## Bullet chat

- Bullet chat is larger by default (52 instead of 44), and the size slider now goes up to 96. A size you chose yourself is kept.
- The area choices (top quarter, top half, full screen) wrap onto a second line when their names run long, instead of breaking words letter by letter.
