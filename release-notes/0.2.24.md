## Bullet chat

- Emoji show in bullet chat: YouTube's own emoji (such as the smiling faces and channel member emoji) as their pictures instead of their `:shortcut:` names, and emoji typed on a phone in colour.

## Live streams

- A live stream above 1080p no longer drops to 1080p after a seek. Seeking inside what has already loaded works as before; a seek further ahead goes to the newest moment, and one further back stays put with a note, since this quality cannot go back that far.
- A live stream above 1080p that breaks right after opening is tried once more at its quality before it falls back to 1080p.

## Subtitles

- The subtitle list shows the subtitles on screen: after the second subtitle was turned on by itself, or the first one was turned off again, it no longer stays empty or keeps showing both.
- The second subtitle is no longer taken for off once a translation of it finishes, and choosing a subtitle that is loaded already no longer loads it twice.
