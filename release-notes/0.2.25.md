## Explore

- The language filter offers all 72 interface languages, 35 more than before.
- "Confirmed audio only" is on by default and stricter: a video counts only when its original audio track or automatic captions are in the language. The original track is found from YouTube's own audio tags, whatever the interface language, and a dub YouTube made the default no longer passes for it. A video whose language could not be looked up is left out.
- Popular, long videos and the most-watched live streams are ranked by views, and live streams by the people watching now, which their cards show. Videos keep YouTube's order while their language is being checked. With a language picked, videos show as they are found instead of after a page and a half of lookups.
- Global trending is gone; All with a region or language picked covers it.
- Live zones in a new order, with Lifestyle & fashion, Food & cooking, Art & creativity, Finance & business and Health & fitness added, Tech & development in place of Tech events, and 24/7 live holding the sleep radio and nature cams.

## Warm light

- The warm light is set as a colour temperature, from 6000 K down to candlelight at 1900 K, as in night light tools such as f.lux.
- Following the sun, it warms with the sun's height through dusk and cools through dawn, from 3° above the horizon to 6° below, so it takes as long as twilight does where you are. On the hours set, it changes over the hour around them. Either way in twelve small steps.
- It warms white and near-white pages too, so the theme presets get warm along with the video, not only the palettes.

## Network

- A proxy auto-config (PAC) script set in Windows, macOS or GNOME is followed: its proxy is used for Lumtub's connections.
- With a SOCKS proxy, host names are looked up by the proxy everywhere, and videos play through Lumtub's relay as with an HTTP proxy, live streams above 1080p included.
- Casting works in full quality through a SOCKS proxy.
- Casting at 720p and above works again: joining the picture and its sound failed on every try, leaving only a single file YouTube rarely serves.
- Deno installs and updates when GitHub's API limit is reached.
