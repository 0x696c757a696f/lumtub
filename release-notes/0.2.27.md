## Playback

- Less memory while watching: what was already played is kept up to the 16–50 MB set aside for seeking back, as intended. libmpv lent that part the unused forward cache too, so a long video or live stream held up to about 190 MB of it.
- The "Low resource use first" playback strategy reads at most 30 seconds and 64 MB ahead and keeps 16 MB of what was played. The default strategy is unchanged.

## Channels

- Channel pictures show on the watch page, on a channel's page and on its posts, where the first letter of the name stood. Each is looked up once and kept.

## Bullet chat

- On videos without a chat, comments that name a moment ("1:23 the best part") fly across the picture at that moment. The first pages of comments are read; a comment listing chapters is left out. It can be turned off in the bullet chat settings.

## Chat and comments

- An emoji button next to the live chat box, comments, replies and post comments. In a live chat the channel's own emoji come first; ones for members only are greyed out.
- Super Chats and Super Stickers show in their amount's colour, as on YouTube, and the larger ones stay in a strip over the live chat for as long as their amount buys; tap one to read it. The heart button over the chat turns both off.
- Chat authors show their picture; tapping a picture or name in the chat, comments or post comments opens that channel.
- Super Chat and Super Thanks: a button next to the live chat box and the comment box opens the video in your browser to pay there. Lumtub never handles payments.
