## Keyboard and mouse

- Player keys can be changed in Settings › Playback settings › Customize shortcuts: seeking, volume, speed, fullscreen, next and previous video, subtitle lines and the rest. An action can have several keys, and a key given to one action leaves the action that had it, so `[` and `]` can seek and `Shift + 9` / `Shift + 0` (the `(` `)` keys) can change the volume. 0-9 and the media keys stay as they are. The shortcut help and the seek and volume settings show the keys in use.
- Right click on the video can leave fullscreen, or else go back to the page before (Settings › Playback settings › Right click on the video, off by default).
