## Explore

- Explore sub-categories, live ones with a language picked in particular, no longer load for a long time and end empty: the list keeps reading until videos actually show, videos whose language YouTube does not report are kept, and a failed source shows an error instead of an empty list.
- Zone mixes read their searches in parallel, so they fill faster. Live mixes search whole zones and keep streams you already watched.
- Only Chinese, Japanese and Korean words of a zone search are translated; words like "lofi" or "blender" stay as they are, and 直播 becomes "live".

## Linux

- The AppImage now runs on Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 36 and newer (glibc 2.35 or later).
- It carries libmpv 0.35.1 built for it, which also plays on machines without a working graphics driver.
