## Explore

- Explore sub-categories, live ones with a language picked in particular, no longer load for a long time and end empty: the list keeps reading until videos actually show, videos whose language YouTube does not report are kept, and a failed source shows an error instead of an empty list.
- Zone mixes read their searches in parallel, so they fill faster. Live mixes search whole zones and keep streams you already watched.
- Live streams show how many people are watching now, apart from their view count.
- Upcoming streams and replays look through more pages before they stop, and an empty sub-category searches its zone more widely.
- When videos were found but your filters left all of them out, the page says so and offers to keep looking.
- Only Chinese, Japanese and Korean words of a zone search are translated; words like "lofi" or "blender" stay as they are, and 直播 becomes "live".

## Linux

- The AppImage now runs on Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 36 and newer (glibc 2.35 or later).
- It carries libmpv 0.35.1 built for it, which also plays on machines without a working graphics driver.
- An AppImage in a folder Lumtub cannot write, such as one a package manager installed, is left for that package manager to update.

## Block lists

- The block lists are now plain text files in the repository, each with its sources, read by the Rust core.
- New lists of state media from Vietnam, Azerbaijan, Eritrea, Myanmar and Turkmenistan, and more channels in the Russian, Chinese and religious lists.
- A new install asks once which block lists to turn on; none is on unless you tick it. Settings saved by an earlier version stay as they were.
- Lumtub is also packaged for WinGet, Scoop and the AUR.
