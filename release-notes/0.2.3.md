## Lumtub 0.2.3

### Changed
- **Smoother lists and comments.** Comments in the side panel and under community posts are built only as they scroll into view, and comment block checks are remembered. Home, search, Explore and playlist pages no longer filter their whole list again on every redraw, and the Shorts test is remembered per video.
- **Less work on the interface's thread.** A community post's page is read on a background thread, and DeArrow answers go to the native core as they arrive instead of being escaped into JSON first.

### Added
- **Settings › Recommendations › Auto-refresh For you.** On by default (as before: For you is gathered again at launch and once it is 30 minutes old). Off, the last list stays until you press Refresh.

### Fixed
- **Subtitles and bilingual subtitles.** Captions are now fetched by Lumtub and handed to the player as a file. When YouTube answers "too many requests" (429), Lumtub waits and tries again, and a translation YouTube keeps refusing is made from the original track with the same translator as the Translate button.
- **Self-update that did not install.** The installer now goes into the folder Lumtub runs from, asks for administrator rights only when that folder needs them, and its log is kept, so a failed update shows up in Lumtub's log.
- **For you kept from the last launch** now continues when you scroll to its end, also with auto-refresh off.
- **Shorts from followed channels in For you.** A channel's feed now marks its Shorts, so the Shorts setting for recommendations (reduce or hide) also applies to Shorts longer than a minute.

### Performance
- **Up next** uses the suggestions YouTube sends with the video page Lumtub already loads, instead of a separate search on the title.
- **System proxy** is read from the registry by the native core instead of starting `reg.exe` twice on almost every play.

### Downloads
| Package | For |
|---|---|
| `x64-v3` | CPUs with AVX2, most from 2015 on |
| `x64` | Older x64 CPUs |
| `arm64` | Windows on ARM |

## Lumtub 0.2.3（中文）

- **列表和评论更流畅**：侧栏和社区帖子下的评论改为滚动到哪里才构建到哪里，评论的屏蔽判断会记住结果。首页、搜索、探索和播放列表页不再每次重绘都把整个列表重新过滤一遍，Shorts 判断按视频记住结果。
- **界面线程上的工作更少**：社区帖子页面改到后台线程读取；DeArrow 的回复直接交给原生核心，不再先转义成 JSON。
- **新增设置：推荐 › 自动刷新“为你推荐”**：默认开启（和以前一样，启动时和列表超过 30 分钟后重新获取）；关闭后保留上次的列表，点刷新才更新。
- **修复**：关注频道的 Shorts 现在能被识别，超过一分钟的 Shorts 也会按“推荐中的 Shorts 短视频”设置降低或隐藏。
- **修复字幕和双语字幕**：字幕改由 Lumtub 自己下载后交给播放器。YouTube 返回“请求太多”（429）时会等一下再试；YouTube 一直拒绝自动翻译时，改为下载原文字幕，用和“翻译”按钮相同的翻译服务逐句翻译。
- 关闭自动刷新时，上次保留的“为你推荐”滚动到底也能继续加载。
- **修复自动更新没装上**：安装程序现在装到 Lumtub 正在运行的目录，目录需要管理员权限时才请求；安装日志会保留，更新失败会写进 Lumtub 的日志。
- **接下来播放** 直接用 YouTube 随视频页返回的相关视频，不再按标题另外搜索一次。
- **系统代理** 改由原生核心直接读注册表，不再几乎每次播放都启动两次 `reg.exe`。
