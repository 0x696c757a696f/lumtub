## Skins

- New: skins that change Lumtub's whole look, in Settings › Appearance › Skins. 23 come built in: Y2K and Frutiger Aero styles with painted day and night scenes, retro desktops, early phone looks and more. The standard look stays the default.
- The ten colour schemes are skins now too, listed under Colour schemes on the same page; a scheme you had chosen carries over.
- A skin can draw its own title bar and menu bar. The empty part of either moves the window, and on Windows a double click maximizes and the maximize button shows snap layouts. "Hide the title bar" puts the window buttons into the menu bar; "Use the system title bar" turns both bars off.
- Glass skins can be made clearer, and some let the desktop show through the window (blurred on Windows and macOS). This is new, so please report how it looks on your system.
- Each skin is marked light and dark, light only or dark only, and the theme mode can be changed at the top of the page.
- Anyone can make a skin: a `skin.jsonc` file in the skins folder, with a commented template ("New skin"), a guide and a schema for editors. Changes show as soon as the file is saved.
- Every text and icon colour is checked for contrast, over pictures and glass too, and fixed if too faint. Skins follow warm light and Low resource mode.

## Android (preview)

- New: Lumtub for Android phones, tablets and TVs (Android 7.0 or newer). Pick the `android-arm64-v8a` APK unless your device is an older 32-bit one.
- Background playback with media controls in the notification, picture in picture when you leave a playing video, and the back button going through pages.
- Phones get a bottom bar, with History, Playlists and Downloads together under Library. On Android TV the remote moves through everything and OK plays and pauses.
- Skins work on every device, each can look different on phone, tablet and TV, and you can add one from a file. On phones and tablets, glass skins can show your wallpaper through the app.
- Not yet on Android: live streams, members-only videos and downloads, because yt-dlp doesn't run there. Lumtub tells you about new versions, but you install them yourself.
- The APKs carry a post-quantum signature (ML-DSA-65 alongside EC P-384) as well as the usual one.

## Casting

- AirPlay (preview): Lumtub now finds Apple TVs and TVs with AirPlay and casts to them. The first time, the TV shows a PIN to type into Lumtub; after that it remembers the TV. Play, pause, seeking and stop work from Lumtub. This is new, so please report how it works on your TV.
- Each TV can have its own stream format: MPEG-TS, HLS, MP4 or MKV, from the ⋮ menu next to it in the cast window. Auto picks HLS for Apple devices, MPEG-TS for other DLNA TVs and MP4 for Chromecast.
- Subtitles travel inside HLS and MKV streams, so TVs that ignore separate subtitle files show them too, one language or two.

## Subtitles

- Subtitle lines on screen are evener: at most two lines, split to similar lengths, shrinking a little before wrapping, and never wider than 90% of the picture.

## Live chat and support

- The Super Chat button opens the live chat's own window, where YouTube takes the payment, and a channel's menu has Join as a member.

## Diagnostics

- Settings has a Diagnostics page: a network check, a short playback test of H.264, VP9 and AV1 with CPU, memory and dropped frames, and what is playing now (decoder, hardware or software, dropped frames).

## Player

- The More menu can rotate the picture by 90°, 180° or 270°, for videos filmed sideways. Like mirror and zoom, it resets for the next video.

## Linux

- The AppImage now carries libmpv 0.41 with FFmpeg 9 and libplacebo, up from libmpv 0.35 with Ubuntu 22.04's FFmpeg 4.4: newer hardware decoding (AV1 in particular) and fewer bundled libraries.

## macOS

- The bundled ffmpeg is now FFmpeg 9.0.1, a ready-made LGPL build (dfloresc/ffmpeg-macos-builds) for each chip instead of one built for both: Intel Macs get the faster assembly code it had to leave out, and the DMGs are a little smaller.

## Fixes

- Holding the mouse for 2× speed while the video changed left the next video fast; it now plays at the speed set before.
- Newer emoji in live chat and danmaku no longer show as empty boxes on systems whose emoji font lacks them (such as Windows 10): YouTube's own pictures of them are used.
- A channel with no Videos or Shorts tab shows an empty list instead of an error.
- Downloading yt-dlp, Deno or FFmpeg no longer fails at once when GitHub is briefly overloaded (HTTP 429 or 502–504): it asks again a few times.

- DLNA: TVs and boxes using Lebo (乐播) receivers can be cast to again.
- Linux: the video no longer stays black with Mesa graphics drivers.
- Linux: on a system with no PipeWire server running, playback no longer crashes trying it; sound goes through PulseAudio or ALSA.
- Turning danmaku on with nothing playing no longer fails.
- The sidebar labels follow a change of interface language right away.
- Explore: live stream lists are fetched again after two minutes instead of fifteen, and a search that finds only channels falls back to yt-dlp for videos.
