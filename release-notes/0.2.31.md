## Block lists

- Lumtub no longer asks about block lists when it starts, and nothing is blocked until you turn a list on in Settings. The question also came back at some starts and replaced the choices you had made; if you use block lists, please check them in Settings.
- Block lists now update on their own: once a day Lumtub fetches the newest lists, checks their signature (the same keys as Lumtub's own updates) and uses them from the next start. Settings › Home & search settings › Update block lists automatically shows the date of the lists in use and turns this off.
- The block list pages have a button that opens a form for suggesting a channel or reporting one listed by mistake. How the lists work and how to contribute is in [lists/README.md](https://github.com/0x696c757a696f/lumtub/blob/main/lists/README.md).

## Skins

- Glass goes down to 30% on every glass skin, Aurora glass included. Below the clearest level a skin keeps fully readable, the colour behind the video lists thins too, so more of the picture shows.
- Settings pages draw their title bar in the skin's style, glass included.
- "See the desktop through the window" is gone. In its place, Skin background turns a skin's pictures and landscape off and leaves a plain colour.
- 1-bit: the checkered desk shows only faintly behind the pages, so text there reads.

## Theater mode

- In a window at least 900 pixels wide, the video now fills the height and comments, up next and chat sit in a side panel on the right with tabs. The panel folds away to give the video the whole width.

## Releases

- Release builds no longer run the tests and screenshots their pull request already ran, so a new version is out sooner.
