## Android

- yt-dlp now runs on Android, with the Python that comes inside the app. Live streams, members-only videos and downloads work, and a video Lumtub's own reader cannot open is tried again with yt-dlp.
- Downloads keep the best sound without ffmpeg: Lumtub joins the picture and sound itself. Opus sound gives a WebM file (an MKV beside H.264), AAC and Dolby (AC-3, E-AC-3) an MP4. Saved files carry their length and an index, so they seek at once.
- Lumtub updates itself: it downloads the new APK, checks its signature and hands it to Android's installer. Over a metered connection it only offers the update.
- On a TV, the screen switches to a refresh rate that fits the video while it plays (Settings › Playback › Match the frame rate), pages keep clear of the screen edges, and text is a little larger.
- Before looking for cast devices, Lumtub asks for Android 17's local network permission.

## Block lists

- 96 more channels in the block lists are matched by their channel id, so they stay blocked when they change their name, and 17 channels that were listed twice are now one entry each.
