## Video cards

- Cards show when a video came out ("3 days ago"), in every interface language.
- Explore's most watched lists also offer "Any time", so older good videos are not left out.

## Downloads

- No more ffmpeg: Lumtub joins picture and sound itself on every platform, into the MP4, WebM or MKV picked in the download dialog. The ffmpeg an earlier version installed is removed.
- Downloaded MP4 and MKV files carry their subtitles (with their languages), the title and the cover inside.
- SponsorBlock › Cut them out of downloads leaves the chosen segments out of downloaded videos, cut at keyframes without encoding again. Subtitles, chapters and the saved segments move with the cut. Off by default.
- Live streams can be recorded: the record button on a live's watch page or in a card's menu records the best the playback settings allow, up to 4K. Downloads shows the size so far and stops it; the recording is saved as one file, also when the live ends or Lumtub was closed while recording.

## Speech recognition

- Long videos are cut into parts by Lumtub itself, without ffmpeg.
