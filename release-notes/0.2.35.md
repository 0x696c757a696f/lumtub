## New

- Import your follow list from a NewPipe, FreeTube, Google Takeout or OPML file, without signing in (Subscriptions page or Settings).
- Channel videos, Shorts and live streams can be sorted by latest, most popular or oldest.
- Copy a link that starts at the current time, from the watch page.
- Skip silence (Settings › Playback): quiet stretches play at 4×.
- Hide members-only videos (off by default).
- Ten more interface languages: Serbian (Cyrillic and Latin), Croatian, Slovenian, Bosnian, Catalan, Kazakh, Kyrgyz, Assamese and Odia, also as subtitle and translation targets. 82 in all.
- Cast quality offers 720p and 2K (1440p) next to 1080p, 4K and 8K.
- Cards in history and on the home page show when you watched a video.
- Optional Sunlight app icon (Settings › Appearance › App icon).
- Afdian (Alipay and WeChat Pay) is listed under Support development; now and then, at most every two months and never during playback, Lumtub asks whether you'd like to support it. "Don't ask again" or Settings › About turns it off.

## Privacy and security

- Post-quantum encryption: HTTPS connections Lumtub makes itself offer X25519MLKEM768 first (Settings › Network › Post-quantum encryption: Prefer, Off or Require).
- New switch: fall back to Piped or Invidious when YouTube refuses a video. On by default as before; turn it off and no third party learns what you watch.
- Fast start now always picks the original audio, never an AI-dubbed track YouTube marks as default.
- Several hardening fixes in the core, proxy settings and updater.

## Fixes

- Upload dates show on channel pages again, and in related videos (#77).
- Tapping a commenter opens their channel again when YouTube gives only their @handle.
- With the title bar hidden, its menus open after a restart (#78).
- Right-to-left languages: the side panel, chapter menu, player buttons and card actions follow the reading direction.
- With close or minimize to tray on, the tray icon stays while the window is open too.
- Windows: the packages carry the Visual C++ runtime (#72); the installer warns before a downgrade.
- Linux and macOS remember the window size.

## Installing

- Windows: nothing extra is needed now.
- Linux: if the AppImage says FUSE is missing, install your distribution's `fuse3` package, or start it with `--appimage-extract-and-run`.
- macOS and Android need nothing extra; see the README for the first-open steps.
