## Fixes

- Subtitles drawn by mpv (libass): the second subtitle now moves up when the main one runs onto two or more lines, so the two no longer overlap.
- Turning off mpv-drawn subtitles while a video plays shows the subtitles at once; before, they stayed hidden until a restart.
- Switching the log level to Debug now includes libmpv's own notes straight away, without a restart.

## Performance

- Bullet chat: emoji pictures are kept in a bounded cache and freed when no longer needed, and paused bullet chat no longer redraws 30 times a second.
- Skin background pictures are decoded at the size they are shown; a large photo as background takes far less memory.
- Android: notification and lock-screen artwork is decoded at a size that fits, not at full size.
- The stats overlay and the macOS battery check no longer hold up the interface.

## Block lists

- More NTD and Epoch Times channels, all with checked channel ids; four handles YouTube no longer has were removed.
