## New

- Menu bar on every skin, arranged as File, View, Play, Tools and Help. The Play menu appears only while a video is playing and holds pause, next, screenshot, copy link at the current time, watch later, download, cast and more.
- Settings are split into smaller groups (Playback, Quality & decoding, Sound, Picture, Subtitles, Controls, Network, Recommendations, Filters, Subscriptions, Enhancements, Privacy, Style, Storage, Account, About), so no group is crowded.
- Settings › Quality & decoding › Graphics card (Windows): follow Windows, power saving (integrated) or high performance (dedicated). The interface, video drawing and hardware decoding all move to the chosen card after a restart.

## Fixes

- Title bar menus respond from the moment the app starts; before, a click on a menu could start dragging the window instead.
- Views and upload time show again on videos where YouTube writes the view count as a bare number.
- Clearing history or caches can no longer be undone by a save that was still under way.
- macOS: the video no longer forces the discrete GPU on Intel Macs with two graphics cards.

## Performance

- Linux: each video frame waits on a GPU fence instead of stalling the whole pipeline, which lowers CPU use while playing.
- Thumbnails, chat replays, buffers and proxy connections now have size limits, and start-up and shutdown free everything they create.
