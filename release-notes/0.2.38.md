## New

- Settings › Translation › Take Simplified and Traditional Chinese as one language (on by default): Chinese in either script is no longer translated when the target is the other.

## Fixes

- Video cards from subscription feeds and the signed-in home page now show length, views and upload time. What a listing leaves out is asked of YouTube once per video and kept on this computer.
- The signed-in home page reads a video's length and upload time from the card's text when the thumbnail carries none.
- Subtitles move up above the player controls while they show, so the bottom line is no longer covered, and come back down when the controls hide.
- With subtitles drawn by mpv (libass), the second subtitle no longer leaves an empty line above the first: line widths are now estimated letter by letter.
