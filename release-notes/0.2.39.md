## New

- Settings › Translation › Languages never translated: pick the languages you read, and immersive translation of comments, posts and live chat leaves text in them alone.
- Subtitles translated by a chosen service (DeepL, AI and the like) are translated only around where you are watching, five minutes ahead, and follow playback; the whole track is translated only when it is saved or downloaded. A long video watched in part costs far fewer requests.
- AI translation sends only the glossary terms the text actually uses.
- "Skip text already in the target language" now also recognises Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Vietnamese and other Latin-script languages once a line is long enough.

## Fixes

- Short lines sent for translation together are batched into one request, so comments and live chat translate faster and hit rate limits less.
- Settings pages opened over a skin's background picture (Bubble Gel, Ocean, Meadow, City, Aquarium) get the same veil as the main page, so their text reads clearly.
- Windows: with the app's own title bar, the window's content is refitted after the frame changes, aiming at menus that did not highlight on hover until the window was maximized once.
