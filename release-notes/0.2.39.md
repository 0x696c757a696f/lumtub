## New

- Android phones: the watch page puts the player at the top with Overview, Up next, Comments, Chat and Subtitles in tabs below it; turning the phone sideways goes full screen. The mini player is a bar above the navigation, sliding the player up enters full screen and down shrinks it, menus open as bottom sheets, and player buttons are finger-sized.
- Skins work on phones, tablets and TVs.
- Android picks VP9 or AV1 and qualities above 1080p automatically when the phone decodes them in hardware.
- Newer libmpv everywhere: Linux packages are now built from mpv's latest code with FFmpeg 9, VA-API, VDPAU and NVDEC; Android uses a newer mpv build; macOS gets mpv 0.41 with FFmpeg 9 and AV1 hardware decoding.
- New Linux x64-v3 AppImage for CPUs with AVX2 (most from 2015 on): the app core and libmpv are built for them. Pick it if unsure; on older CPUs it says so and you can use the plain x64 one.
- Subtitles: under libass the second subtitle sits right above the first and its size can be set; playback waits up to 60 seconds for a translation, and a subtitle that could not be opened is tried again.
- Settings › Translation › Languages never translated: pick the languages you read, and immersive translation of comments, posts and live chat leaves text in them alone.
- Subtitles translated by a chosen service (DeepL, AI and the like) are translated only around where you are watching, five minutes ahead, and follow playback; the whole track is translated only when it is saved or downloaded. A long video watched in part costs far fewer requests.
- AI translation sends only the glossary terms the text actually uses.
- "Skip text already in the target language" now also recognises Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Vietnamese and other Latin-script languages once a line is long enough.

## Fixes

- Short lines sent for translation together are batched into one request, so comments and live chat translate faster and hit rate limits less.
- Settings pages opened over a skin's background picture (Bubble Gel, Ocean, Meadow, City, Aquarium) get the same veil as the main page, so their text reads clearly.
- Windows: with the app's own title bar, the window's content is refitted after the frame changes, aiming at menus that did not highlight on hover until the window was maximized once.
- Windows: starting Lumtub again brings up the window that is already running instead of opening a second copy; restart waits for the old one to close.
- The video output is created when the first video opens, not while the window sits idle.
- Less work in the background: home feed expansion, process output, download folders and cast status checks were made lighter.
