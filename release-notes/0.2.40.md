## New

- Phones: the watch page is laid out as phone video apps have it: channel first, the title folded to two lines, the main actions as icons over their counts, and what plays next right below. The small player has one thin bar; full screen keeps the picture clear with like, comments, favorite and share by the title. Double tap in the middle plays or pauses, taps at the sides seek further, and vertical videos go full screen upright.
- Tablets held upright get the phone watch page with tabs. TVs play full screen, fit three or more cards a row, have no top bar (search and settings sit in the side rail), and the remote plays with OK, seeks with left and right and walks the player's buttons with the arrows. Filters and chips on TV sit on one line that scrolls sideways.
- Phone channel page: tabs in one row pinned at the top and a compact header.
- Pull to refresh on every list page; a second press of a page's button goes to its top and then refreshes; F5 and Ctrl+R refresh. Home keeps the videos seen before below a line that says where they begin.
- Shortcuts from YouTube and Bilibili (subtitles on or off, subtitle size, mini player, bullet chat, chapters) and page keys (Home, End, Space, Shift+Space, /, ?), all customizable.
- Cards as Bilibili lays them out: views and age over the foot of the thumbnail, then the title, then one line with the reason, the channel and the menu.
- Explore gains an Academia zone: the humanities, social sciences, natural sciences, engineering, medicine and academic live, with many sub-categories.
- Comments the creator liked show the creator's picture with a heart; replies sit on a faint tint of the page.
- Automatic captions are translated by whole sentences, and each translation shows for its whole sentence's time; the subtitle list shows the sentence as one row.
- Theme mode and the app's accent color moved onto the skins page.
- All 82 interface languages got the newest strings.

## Fixes

- Less memory: the read-ahead cache is 100 MB on computers and 64 MB on phones and TVs (untouched 150 MB settings move over), fewer decoded thumbnails are kept, and fewer resolved streams are held, all let go when the window is hidden with nothing playing.
- libmpv's bare "Failed to open" is no longer reported as a network failure: a line naming the cause is shown first, or the message points to Settings › Network › Network check.
- A tap on Translate turns Traditional Chinese into Simplified (and back).
- Portrait videos' quality is named by their width, as YouTube names it: 1080p, not 1920p.
- Skin pictures no longer blink out for a frame when the window rebuilds.
- Hiding vertical videos holds for every Explore list.
- Card facts are found through search when YouTube wants sign-in for a video's details.
- Comment @handles and YouTube's own emoji in comments are found more reliably.
