## New

- iPhone and iPad (preview, not yet tested on a real device): an unsigned IPA for SideStore, AltStore, Sideloadly or TrollStore. Add the source https://github.com/0x696c757a696f/lumtub/releases/latest/download/altstore-source.json for updates. yt-dlp runs inside the app with an embedded Python.
- Related videos can be narrowed as on YouTube: all, from this channel, related content, for you, recently uploaded and watched. Next to a live, a Live chip shows other lives.
- Academia gets a Frontier research tab: this year's seminars, talks and papers, by discipline. The video and live halves of Explore show only their own tabs.
- Dubbed audio on every system: the original track plays by default even when YouTube marks a dub as default, an optional dub in your language, and an audio language choice per video.
- Comments the creator hearted show the heart with the creator's picture and a "Hearted by" name.
- Every card's menu can open the channel.
- Pressing the tab already shown goes back to the top: watch page tabs, channel tabs and post comments.
- The error log can be exported as a text file.
- New skin Azure Tomorrow: glass towers by a clear lake with a maglev train and soap bubbles, by day and under the moon at night.
- Theme presets sit with the color schemes on the skins page; App theme became Custom color, with the custom color, system accent and palette style.

## Fixes

- Video cards grow with the text size, so the second title line is no longer cut off; recommendation reasons get their own line and read in full.
- Playback stats name the video codec on every system.
- Android player: subtitles sized by the player's height with system fonts per script, a tap shows the controls, double tap pauses, sliding seeks.
- The next video is resolved only once playback has settled, and thumbnails for cards that left the screen are no longer downloaded.
- Every redirect hop is checked against the first request's host rules.
