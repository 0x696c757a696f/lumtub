## New

- Smoother motion throughout: switching between light and dark fades across, the bottom bar and side rail slide in and out, pages fade and slide a little when you change tabs, and navigation icons fill in when selected.
- Panels such as comments and the AI summary open from the side in a wide window and from the bottom on a phone.
- Explore copes with long lists: topics and subtopics stay on one line that scrolls, fades at the edge and keeps your choice in view, with a button that lists them all.
- On a phone, Explore's sort orders fit on one line and the filters open from a button that shows how many are set.
- Ko-fi is a new way to support Lumtub, for one-time gifts by PayPal or card without a GitHub account.

## Fixes

- Explore's field tabs (Humanities, Frontier research and so on) are never cut off at the window's edge; when they do not fit side by side they become text tabs.
- Loading placeholders wait a moment before showing, so quick loads no longer flash.
- Less redrawing while scrolling and playing, which helps slower phones.
