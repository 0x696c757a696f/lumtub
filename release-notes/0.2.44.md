## New

- Explore: tapping All shows every category as a grid.
- Subtitles drawn by libmpv now look like the normal subtitle view: same size, position, outline and colours, and the second subtitle has its own font, colour and opacity. Long lines in Chinese, Japanese, Thai and other scripts written without spaces wrap instead of running off the edges.
- Smooth mouse-wheel scrolling on desktop: each notch glides to its place, and quick notches add up.
- A smoother feel everywhere: loaded content fades in, cards sink a little when pressed, and phones give a light tick on taps and long presses.
- iOS: browser sign-in opens Google's page inside Lumtub, as on Android.

## Fixes

- Explore with a language picked shows only videos whose audio or captions are in that language; the "Confirmed audio only" switch is gone, as it is now always the case. Videos without captions are judged by their original title and description. Live streams with a language picked no longer come back empty, and languages already worked out are remembered for a week, so the list opens fast.
- YouTube no longer refuses the language check and quick start as a bot.
- A long translated sentence is shown in the lines of the original, each at its own time, instead of as one big block for up to 30 seconds.
- With libmpv subtitles, the second subtitle keeps its colour and background while seeking and while a translation grows.
- Links in descriptions and comments are readable in every skin, and English text with a link is no longer "translated" into itself.
- Filter labels are readable in the dark Cassette and Paper skins.
- Smoother scrolling in video grids and at the end of the home feed.
- Android: Google sign-in in the browser no longer loses its way back to Lumtub.
