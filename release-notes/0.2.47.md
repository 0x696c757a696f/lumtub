## Changes

- Android and iOS: live streams play again where the previous version said "YouTube gave no live stream this version can play". Live streams are now also asked for in a way that needs no JavaScript on the phone.
- Android and iOS: videos with dubbed audio tracks play their original sound again instead of a dub. Desktop could pick a dub the same way when the quick start answered first; that is fixed too.
- Settings > Subtitles: turn subtitles off in picture in picture and in the mini player; smaller subtitles in Android picture in picture; the fullscreen button turns the phone sideways again, and an upright fullscreen no longer cuts off the controls.
