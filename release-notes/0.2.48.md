## Changes

- Theater mode on computers hides the side rail, and the top bar slides in only when the mouse reaches the top edge of the window or you search. The player spans the whole width with no margins at the sides, and the side panel folds open from a button in the player instead of a strip at the right (#113).
- Fill the window: the video takes the whole window without going fullscreen, from a button beside fullscreen, the W key or a right click on the video. Settings > Controls > Right click on the video can still go back instead, or do nothing.
