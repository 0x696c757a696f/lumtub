## Changes

- Fill the window now hides the system title bar too, so only the video is left in the window; leaving brings it back.
- Settings > Appearance > Hide the side rail while watching: the rail steps aside on the video page in normal mode as well, as on YouTube. Theater mode always hides it.
- Smoother motion: fill the window fades and grows into the window; theater mode's side panel slides in and out with the player following it; the top bar folds away on entering theater mode; thumbnails and channel pictures fade in once loaded; search suggestions drop in; comment replies grow open; the mini player settles into place. With animations reduced, all of it switches at once.
