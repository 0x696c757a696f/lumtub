## Lumtub 0.2.5

### Updates
- **Updates signed with a key the app doesn't know are skipped, not downloaded again and again.** The settings page says so and opens the release page instead.
- **A failed download says why and can be retried** from Settings › About.

### Subtitles
- **Subtitle style applies live.** The player's subtitle settings open a side panel: font, size, colour, opacity, position, outline, background and bold change on the video while you drag.
- **Bilingual subtitle list.** With two subtitles on, each line shows both languages; choose both (either on top), the first only or the second only. The list follows subtitle changes at once.
- **Subtitle translation by sentence.** Caption fragments are joined into whole sentences before translating, so a sentence is never split between two requests and the translated subtitle shows whole sentences. AI translation now gets the three sentences before each request as context.
- **The selected first and second subtitles show correctly** in the subtitle panel, and choosing one that is already on the other side swaps them.
- **The second subtitle is a little larger** by default (0.9 of the first).

### Translation
- **Titles and descriptions in your language** when the uploader provided a translation.
- **Mixed-language comments.** Translating sends only the lines not already in the target language.

### Playback
- **Changing a setting no longer restarts the video decoder.** Only the options that changed are applied.
- **Fewer texture swaps.** Opening a page over the player keeps the video's size.
- **A stalled picture comes back by itself.** If no frame reaches the screen for 10 s while the video plays, Lumtub restarts the decoder or re-attaches the picture, and logs it.

### Comments
- **Roomier reply box** spanning the panel, with its buttons below it.
