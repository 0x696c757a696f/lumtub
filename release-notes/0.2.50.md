## Changes

- Faster video starts: yt-dlp asks as the visionOS client by default, which needs no JavaScript challenge and gives up to 2160p, so a click on a thumbnail no longer waits 10 seconds or more. YouTube's other clients are used for a while only when that fails (#132).
- In fullscreen the mouse pointer hides at once, without waiting for the mouse to move (#132).
- Window transparency: Settings > Style > Window transparency shows the desktop through the window like glass, under every skin, for the bars only or the whole window, with an optional system blur. Windows, macOS and Linux; on Android the wallpaper shows behind the app (#121).
- Downloads can include subtitles: pick the video's own tracks or YouTube's translation into any language, add a bilingual track with two lines per cue, and save them as SRT if you like. Subtitles in the video are styled like your subtitle settings. A WebM download with subtitles is saved as MKV (#135).
- Updates: the home page tells you when a new Lumtub is ready to install or can be downloaded, and a dot marks Settings. A newer release found before you installed the previous one now replaces it properly (#129).
- Explore language filter: a zone with a language picked goes on with its sub-categories, every keyword is translated from English, your own zones are translated too, and "live" is never translated (#119).
- Back, paging and focus: Back from a video returns to the channel you picked in Subscriptions, and to the page under the mini player; PgUp/PgDn move the list under the mouse; arrow keys and Esc leave a search field; a two-finger swipe goes back on a touchpad; the duration on cards is drawn like the views (#128).
- Player panels: headings fit in every language, and the video and its subtitles move beside an open side panel instead of hiding under it (#130).
- The hardware decoder list shows only decoders this device has: MediaCodec on Android, VideoToolbox on iOS (#136).
- For you: when every recommendation source fails, Lumtub tries once more and says why (#124).
- iPhone and iPad: playback works again after the app was in the background, phones keep playing the sound in the background, and hardware-decoded video no longer shows green and purple colours (#131, #133). Android: Picture in picture when leaving the app is now a setting, off by default.
- Linux Flatpak bundles in every release, signed like the other files; WinGet, Scoop and AUR packages are on their way (#120, #126).
- Security hardening: yt-dlp ignores config files and plugins on the computer, the Windows updater checks the very file it runs, stricter native builds, and parser fixes found by fuzzing (#122).
