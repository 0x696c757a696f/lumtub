## Changes

- iPhone and iPad: signing in to your YouTube account with your own Google client works again; it failed with "Starting new processes is not supported on iOS" (#147).
- Android and iOS: "Restart now" after changing the interface language no longer fails. Android opens Lumtub again; on iOS Lumtub closes and you open it again from the home screen (#147).
- iPhone, iPad and Android phones: video keeps playing as sound when you switch to another app or lock the screen. 0.2.50 still paused it there, because the video view paused on its own when the app went to the background (#147).
- iPhone and iPad: the picture no longer disappears after Lumtub was in the background. iOS ends the hardware video decoder of an app in the background; Lumtub now turns the picture off before that and on again once it is back, and makes a new decoder if one stops working, without reloading the video.
- iPhone and iPad: the playing video shows in Now Playing on the Lock Screen, in Control Center and in the Dynamic Island, with play, pause, seeking, next and previous; headphone and AirPods buttons control it too.
- Settings › Network: Certificate authorities and Post-quantum encryption now also apply to yt-dlp and to live streams. yt-dlp trusts the chosen list and, with Require, refuses servers without X25519MLKEM768 or with only TLS 1.2; live streams up to 1080p go through Lumtub's relay like other videos. With Mozilla, Google or Require, playback no longer falls back to the player's own connections. Pictures in chat, emoji, channel posts and the Downloads page are fetched by Lumtub's own network too.
- Windows installer: started without administrator rights for a folder that needs them, such as D:\Program Files, it asks to start again as administrator instead of stopping on "Access denied" for unins000.exe. It also opens in Simplified Chinese on Chinese Windows and in English for languages it lacks, instead of Japanese (#148).
