## Changes

- iPhone and iPad: picture in picture no longer ends Lumtub after a few seconds. Every frame made for the floating window left a little memory behind on the player's thread until iOS closed the app; it is now given back after each frame, and those frames are at most 720 lines tall.
- iPhone and iPad: Lumtub is no longer ended in the background for not answering, and subtitles no longer make the picture stutter when they appear. Subtitles are drawn in fonts that come with the app (Noto Sans, with a font for each of 20 scripts, Korean, Arabic, Thai and Hindi among them) instead of searching the system's fonts, which on iOS 27 held up the player for seconds.
- The app no longer waits for the player when it changes or reads a setting, so a busy player can't freeze the screen (all platforms).
- Two subtitles: the second one is drawn at its own size again on iPhone and iPad (it came out about half as big and ran into the first), and a newly chosen or translated second subtitle shows straight away in its own colour instead of white first.
- iPhone and iPad: the subtitle font setting names the font really used.
- Phones, tablets and TVs keep less in memory, so the system is less likely to close Lumtub in the background: iPhone and iPad read ahead 64 MB like other phones (they used a computer's 100 MB), devices with 3 GB of memory or less 32 MB, and in the background a phone or tablet reads at most 30 seconds ahead. When the system runs short of memory Lumtub gives back what it can at once.
- Android: after a first video played in the background, Lumtub offers once to run without battery restrictions, as some phones close background apps to save battery even while they play. The same is in Settings (Keep playing in the background).
- iPhone and iPad: plain-text (http) connections outside the local network are no longer allowed, and the sign-in page opens only https pages.
- iPhone and iPad: full-screen controls keep to the picture instead of the black bars beside it; the eye-care warmth no longer covers the picture; software-decoded video shows again.
- Subtitle lines that Lumtub breaks for libmpv leave a little room at the edges and are balanced in length (all platforms).
- Google translation that comes back unchanged is asked again in the language its letters show.
