## Changes

- Window transparency on Windows 10: how thick the frost over the desktop is now follows the Transparency slider, from frosted at the left to see-through at the right. 0.2.52 made it thick at every setting, so the window looked solid.
- Language learning, free for everyone, in the Subtitles menu: **Pause after each line** stops playback as each subtitle line ends (Q); **Blur the translation** keeps the second subtitle blurred until you hover over it, tap it or press V. **Loop this line** (Shift+R) plays the current line over and over. Previous line, Replay this line and Next line (A, R, D) now go by the subtitle list's lines and start playing, and the player bar has buttons for them and for the loop, for touch screens and TV remotes.
- Shadowing: with Pause after each line on, the microphone button in the player bar (or E) records you saying the line, then plays the line and your recording one after the other to compare. The recording stays on the device and is deleted with the next video, unless you save it as a WAV file with the save button next to it. Recording works on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS and Android 8 and later.
- The subtitle list exports every line with its translation as a CSV file, for Anki or a spreadsheet.
