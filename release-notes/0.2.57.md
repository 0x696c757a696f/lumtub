## Changes

- Explore by language finds videos again: picking a language in Explore now fills the page with videos spoken in that language. Lumtub checked its language detection against about 1,600 real videos in all 70 languages: it now finds 97.7% of the videos in the chosen language (up from 90%) and shows none in another language.
- Short titles, titles in two languages, automatic captions that YouTube labels with a neighbouring language, and channels that always speak one language are read more carefully, so Polish, Czech, Slovak, Hungarian, Turkish, Azerbaijani, Estonian, Finnish, Albanian, Basque, Swahili, Persian and other smaller languages are told apart.
- Explore by language is faster: Lumtub checks up to 16 videos at once on computers and 8 on phones and tablets, and pauses checking while the app is in the background on a phone.
- Explore looks further back: **Popular** and **Long videos** now show the most watched videos of all time by default (pick a shorter period in the menu). **Latest**, **Hidden gems** and **New creators** stay recent.
- Searching in Esperanto, Latin, Javanese, Sundanese, Tigrinya, Turkmen and Persian now finds videos in those languages instead of an empty or English page.
- Page Up and Page Down (and the other page keys) work right after Lumtub starts and after a click on Home, Explore, Subscriptions and the other page buttons; until now nothing was focused then on Windows, Linux and macOS, and they did nothing until an arrow key was pressed.
