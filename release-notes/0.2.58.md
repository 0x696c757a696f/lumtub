## New: the learning pack

Learn languages from the videos you watch. Tap any word in a subtitle, on the video or in the subtitle list, and a word card opens with its dictionary entries, reading and the line it came from; the video waits while you read.

- Dictionaries for Chinese, Japanese, Korean, English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Russian and more, downloaded once and used offline.
- Readings above the words: pinyin, furigana (kana) and romanization, on the video and in the subtitle list. Hard words can be highlighted.
- Words read aloud in natural voices (Microsoft Edge's online voices, nothing to set up; the computer's own voice when they can't be reached). Your own Azure or Google key works too.
- A vocabulary book that keeps each word with its line, translation, video and moment; export it as CSV or straight to Anki.
- Everyone can try it: the first 30 lookups have everything on, then 3 lookups a day stay free.
- The full pack is a one-time purchase for up to 5 devices, at a pre-sale price until 3 January 2027. Codes are checked on your device; the activation server keeps no request logs.

## Changes

- Fewer dropped frames while playing with subtitles: each subtitle line is cut into words once, and the subtitle list only redraws the rows that changed.
- Page Up and Page Down (and the other page keys) work right after Lumtub starts and after a click on Home, Explore, Subscriptions and the other page buttons; until now nothing was focused then on Windows, Linux and macOS, and they did nothing until an arrow key was pressed.
- After a week of use, Lumtub asks once whether you'd like to star it on GitHub. It never asks again, waits until nothing is playing, and stays quiet if the support reminder is turned off. About also has a permanent Star it on GitHub entry.
