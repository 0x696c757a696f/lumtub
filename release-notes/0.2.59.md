## Learning pack

- **Review on a memory schedule.** Words in your vocabulary book come back when you are about to forget them (FSRS), each in its own line with the speaker's voice. Grade with Forgot, Hard, Remembered or Easy, or with the keys 1 to 4; the Learning page shows the words due, your days in a row and the words learnt.
- **Words you know.** Mark a word as known and it loses its reading in the subtitles. A line with just one new word gets a small +1 mark.
- **How hard is this video for you?** The learning drawer shows how much of what is said you already know, and the new words said most, to learn first.
- **Your level.** Set your level in Chinese (HSK), Japanese (JLPT N5 to N1), Korean (beginner, intermediate, advanced), or about A1 to C2 in Spanish, French, German, Italian and Russian (estimated from how often words are used, not an official CEFR list). Harder words stand out, and easier ones count as known in a video's difficulty.
- **Vocabulary book.** Tapping a word opens its card and plays its line; the play button beside it jumps to that moment in the video.
- **Pause on hover (computer).** The video pauses while the mouse is on a subtitle line you can look words up in, and plays on when it leaves. Settings › Learning.
- **Learning mode switch.** Not learning a language? Turn learning mode off in Settings › Learning and every learning feature is hidden and stops running. Your words and license are kept.
- **On phones.** The word card rises from the bottom of the screen. Hold a word in a subtitle and drag across others to look up a phrase; swipe across the line to go to the previous or next line. Review cards turn over with a tap and are graded with a swipe. Dictionaries update only on Wi-Fi.
- Japanese words written in kana look up the word meant most (いっぱい is 一杯).
