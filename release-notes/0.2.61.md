## Changes

- **Put your lists in your own order.** Subscription groups can be dragged into order in Manage groups, or sorted by name, number of channels or newest video. Followed channels can be listed by newest video, name, recently followed or most watched. Playlists can be dragged into order or sorted by name, date made or number of videos. A playlist's videos can be sorted by order added, upload date, length or title, turned around, or dragged into order; Play all follows the order shown. Your choices are remembered.
- **Upload a playlist to YouTube.** When you are signed in, your own playlists, Favorites and Watch later have an Upload to YouTube button that makes a private playlist in your account with the same videos in the same order.
- **Reorder your YouTube playlists from Lumtub.** With the browser sign-in, dragging one of your YouTube playlists into order changes it on YouTube too. Only the videos out of place are moved, in a few requests even for long playlists.
- **Picture in picture on iPhone and iPad** now closes when you come back to the app, and the subtitles switch for picture in picture works.
- **Tooltips follow the theme.** In dark mode they are dark, in light mode light, like the menus, instead of the opposite colour. This includes the whole title shown over a cut-off video title.
- **Pointing at a video card no longer turns it white in dark mode** in the Soft Club, Silk Wave and Brutal skins. Soft Club's light side now lights up in a soft mint instead of plain white, and eight more skins keep card text a little more readable while pointed at.
- On phones, a playlist's buttons sit under its title instead of squeezing it.
- Japanese: fixed three mixed-up texts, and private playlists are called 非公開.
