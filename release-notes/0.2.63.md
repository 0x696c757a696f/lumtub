## Changes

- **Videos at your level.** A new Learning page finds videos for the language you are learning, also opened from Explore › Education › Language learning. Graded listening and lessons for your level are free. Native videos are sorted by how much of what is said you already know, from easy to too hard, and each card shows the share of words you know, new words a minute and how fast the speech is. Without the learning pack, the first 8 native videos are shown, then 3 new ones a day.
- **AI overview reads the whole video.** With a hosted model that reads long texts, the whole transcript goes in one request, so chapters and quotes come from what was said. Each chapter has a one-sentence summary, quotes are chosen to be surprising, insightful or memorable, and the chapters now reach the end of the video. Long videos with other models are read three parts at a time.
- **Subtitle list.** Read it in whole sentences, each translated as one. Search shows how many matches there are and steps between them. With a mouse, part of a line can be selected to explain or save.
- **Notes.** Press N to write a note at the current moment; the video pauses while you write. Saved words can be turned into the whole sentence they came from.
- **AI translation keeps lines in place**, even when a model joins them.
- **Immersive translation follows your translation settings.** It uses your target language unless you pick another, asks your chosen service first, and says which service stands in while a free one refuses. Translation layout now also applies to immersive translation and live chat, and changing a setting translates what is shown again.
- Video cards have a new Challenging difficulty band.
