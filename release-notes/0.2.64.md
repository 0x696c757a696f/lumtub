## Changes

- **Top chat or every message.** The live chat panel lets you pick YouTube's Top chat or all messages, and the bullet chat follows your choice. Slow mode, subscribers-only and members-only are shown above the chat. Replays fill in while they load, and messages removed by moderators disappear. In busy chats, at most 40 ordinary messages fly as bullet chat at a time; paid ones always do.
- **Headings fit on phones.** Playlist and channel titles keep their own line and the buttons wrap underneath. Searching on a phone uses the whole top bar, so recent and suggested searches are screen wide.
- **Long YouTube playlists load past the first 100 videos again**, and a channel's own playlists no longer show "Playlist" as their channel.
- Learning: the grey box under Videos at your level is gone.
- iPhone and iPad: the second subtitle in picture in picture is no longer see-through.
