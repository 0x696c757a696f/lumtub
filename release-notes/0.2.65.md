## Changes

- **Titles in your language.** Video and playlist titles on Home, Explore, Subscriptions, History, search, channel and playlist pages are translated into the language you read, on by default. Hover a title to see the original. Settings › Translation › Show › Translate titles in lists lets you turn it off, or show the translation and the original together. Only titles that stay on screen are sent, a batch at a time, and translations are kept on your computer; titles already in your language are left alone.
- **Channel names stay as they are** in lists; on a channel's page, a Translate button shows its name in your language, and immersive translation does so by itself.
- **Explore's language filter is stricter.** A video counts as being in a language only when its text could be written in that language.
