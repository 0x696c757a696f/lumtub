## Changes

- **Screen readers.** Narrator, NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, TalkBack and Orca read each video card as one item: title, live or upcoming, members only, channel, views, age, length, how much you watched and why it is recommended. The card's menu is offered as an action. Page and section titles are headings, and errors, empty pages, loading and seek, speed and volume changes are announced.
- **Player controls stay put when you need them.** They no longer hide while a screen reader runs or while the keyboard focus is in them, and Playback controls display time has a new Never hide choice. The play button is named by what pressing it does.
- **Read subtitles aloud.** A new subtitle setting has the screen reader say each new line.
- **Audio description.** Videos with a described sound track list it in the audio menu as "(audio description)". Turn on Settings › Subtitles › Audio description, or Android's own setting, and it plays first. On Android and iPhone, the system's captions setting turns subtitles on once.
- **Larger text everywhere.** Text size now goes up to 200 %, and pages switch to their narrow layout sooner with large text, on phones, tablets, TVs and desktops.
- **Keyboard and remote.** Tab moves through the top bar, the side bar and the page as groups, coming back from a video puts the focus on its card again, and the focus ring is clear in every palette and skin.
- **Smoother wheel scrolling.** The wheel glides at an even pace, also in low resource mode and with the system's reduced animations; only Lumtub's own Motion setting turns it off. Cards and skin surfaces draw much faster, and when frames run late, effects step back until they catch up.
