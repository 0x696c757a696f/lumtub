## Changes

- **ListenBrainz scrobbling.** Turn it on in Settings › Account and the songs you listen to (YouTube Music tracks, Topic uploads and official music videos) are sent to your ListenBrainz account after half the song or four minutes. Listens made offline are sent later, and nothing is sent in incognito mode.
- **Drag the window by the video.** While the video fills the window, drag the picture to move the window. On Linux the edges still resize it.
- **Middle click on the video.** Choose what it does: nothing, play/pause, mute, fill the window or picture in picture.
- **Channels by length.** A channel's Videos and Live tabs can be ordered Longest or Shortest, and channel tabs and the Subscriptions page have a length filter (under 4, 4 to 20, over 20 minutes). The order you pick on a channel is remembered.
- **Playlists load again.** A YouTube playlist no longer stays on grey placeholders; it fills in as its videos arrive. On phones a playlist shows as numbered rows.
- **Recent searches on phones.** The phone search field shows your recent searches again when you tap it.
- **Quieter notices.** Updater progress, memory warnings and audio filter changes interrupt less.
