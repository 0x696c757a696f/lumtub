## Lumtub 0.2.7

### Live streams
- **Upcoming streams wait instead of failing.** A stream or premiere that has not started showed "can't be watched". It now shows a card with the thumbnail, a countdown and the start time, and plays on its own once the stream begins.

### Subtitles
- **Your subtitle choices carry over.** Turning subtitles on or off and picking the first or second language now apply to the next videos too, as long as they have that subtitle.
- **The panel shows what is really on.** Subtitles that mpv turned on by itself showed as 关闭 in the panel; the panel now reads the subtitle actually playing, for both the first and second subtitle.
- **The video's own language comes first.** In both subtitle lists, the original-language subtitle sits right below 关闭. Settings can also pick it as the first subtitle language.
- **Sentence rows in the subtitle list.** Rows fit their text, and lines covered by one translated sentence become one row, without the blank space.

### Player
- **Autoplay is off by default.** A switch beside 接下来播放 turns it on, and related videos already in the queue are not listed twice.
- **Wheel and slides.** The wheel over the player no longer scrolls the page: on the left half it sets brightness, on the right half the volume. Vertical slides on touch screens do the same.
- **Lock, double tap, rotation, A-B loop.** A lock button for touch screens and full screen; double tap on the left or right third skips 10 s on touch; a rotation lock on phones; A-B loop in the more panel.

### Playback
- **Other servers when YouTube refuses.** Bot checks, 403s and other refusals now also try Piped and Invidious, not only rate limits.
