## Lumtub 0.2.8

### Watching
- **Lights off.** Everything but the player dims (B, the bulb button, or automatically while playing); click the dark area to turn the lights back on. The dim level is in Settings.
- **Ctrl + wheel changes the speed** over the player.
- **Speed and volume per channel.** Each channel plays at the speed and volume you last used for it.
- **Focus mode.** Hide the home feed, comments or related videos.

### AI and subtitles
- **AI summary** of a video from its subtitles, with timestamps that jump to that moment. Long videos are summarized in parts. Without subtitles the summary uses the title, description and chapters.
- **Speech recognition subtitles** for videos that have none: the audio is sent to Groq, OpenAI or any compatible service you set up, and the timed subtitles are kept on this computer. Only the smallest audio stream is downloaded and long videos are cut without re-encoding, so it barely uses the CPU.
- **One place for AI settings.** Settings › 翻译与 AI holds the AI interface shared by AI translation and AI summary, plus speech recognition, each with a test button.

### Library
- **Watch statistics**: today, 7 and 30 days, daily average, a bar per day and your most-watched channels. Kept only on this computer.
- **Subscription groups** to filter the subscriptions page.
- **Playlist length**: total and remaining time in playlist headers.
- **Comment search**, optionally after loading all comments.

### Performance
- Transcript preparation, watch statistics and speech subtitles are worked out in the Rust core on a background thread.
- The upcoming-stream card, the lights-off layer and brightness changes no longer repaint or rebuild the whole page.
