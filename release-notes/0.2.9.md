## Lumtub 0.2.9

### YouTube accounts
- **Settings › YouTube 账号 is simpler.** Each sign-in is a numbered section that says what it is for and whether you are signed in; you only need the one with the feature you want.
- **TV-code sign-in (optional).** Mixes your YouTube home feed into 首页, shows your YouTube watch history, Watch later and account playlists. Sending your watch history back to YouTube is a separate switch and off by default.
- **Browser sign-in (optional)** for liking comments and community posts, voting in polls and commenting under posts. You sign in in an ordinary temporary browser window (Edge, your default Chromium browser, or Chrome); once you close it, the cookies are read and the temporary profile is deleted. They are kept encrypted with the TPM or Windows Hello and never written to the log. A cookies.txt can be imported instead.
- **Like and dislike counts** update right away when you rate a video, comment or post.

### Subtitles and playback
- **Subtitle choices are remembered** when the network drops and comes back and when you change quality: both subtitles, their languages and the display mode stay as you set them.
- The second subtitle carries over to the next video and shows as loading while it is fetched or translated.
- A picture that froze after the video stream broke off (sound still playing) reopens the video at the same place.
- The thin green line at the bottom edge of some hardware-decoded videos is gone.
- Emoji show in live chat and comments.

### Faster start
- The window no longer stalls for most of a second shortly after start.
- The video output is made in the background while you are not using the mouse or keyboard, in about 50 ms instead of several seconds, and only the Direct3D 11 hardware decoding path is loaded.
- Compiled shaders are kept between runs, so videos after the first no longer stutter while they compile.
- A video's first seconds no longer count as "the graphics card can't keep up", which used to switch scaling to the fast kind for the whole run.
- Pages you are not looking at no longer rebuild the window when their data changes.

### Support development
- Settings › 关于 › 支持开发 leads with GitHub Sponsors, followed by crypto addresses with copy buttons.
